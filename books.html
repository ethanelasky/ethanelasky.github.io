On ratings

Scores indicate “do I think should you read this” and not “is this quality”. There is plenty of good art I don’t personally like, and you probably have some gauge of the latter already, so all I can offer is the former.

I try to average ratings at ⭐️⭐️⭐️, following Tabelog’s rating system, to avoid compression.

  • ⭐️: I don’t recommend.
  • ⭐️⭐️/No rating: You won’t miss much by skipping.
  • ⭐️⭐️⭐️ and above: Recommend.

Books I’ve read, most recently finished first; books without a finish date follow. See my shelf on Goodreads.
